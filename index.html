Identified Priorities

Your Challenges & How BigChange Solves Them

Directly addressing the pain points raised during our discussion.

Current Bottleneck

Manual Triple-Handling of Jobs

Calls are taken manually $\rightarrow$ logged into Excel to generate a number $\rightarrow$ re-keyed into Trello $\rightarrow$ transcribed onto paper worksheets.

"It's just too time consuming and takes us away from the proactive stuff."
BigChange Solution

One-Touch Job Creation & Auto-Sequencing

Log the job once. BigChange automatically generates the correct reference (Warranty, Chargeable, Service), plots it on the live schedule, and pushes it directly to the fitter's tablet. Excel and Trello are eliminated.

Current Bottleneck

Zero Labor & Efficiency Visibility

No data on workshop vs. field efficiency. Management cannot reconcile billed hours against actual technician working hours.

"I can't sit there and go: 'I've billed 10 hours, but they've worked 20.' I don't have utilization ability."
BigChange Solution

Live Timesheets Reconciled with GPS Telematics

Engineers clock in on the app. Exact travel time, time on site, and workshop job durations are automatically logged and cross-referenced with vehicle telemetry—giving instant utilization and profitability reports.

Current Bottleneck

Manual Customer Updates During Traffic Delays

Fitters stuck on the motorway (e.g. M6 J12-13) must stop to find customer numbers and call ahead, or customers are left uninformed.

"It would be nice if the system was able to dial ahead and say 9:30 is not going to happen, it's going to be 11:00."
BigChange Solution

Live Traffic Chevrons & Automated Customer Alerts

BigChange factors in live Google traffic. If a fitter is delayed, the office schedule flags it immediately and the system automatically sends a branded SMS/email to the customer with an updated arrival time—hands-free for the fitter.

Current Bottleneck

No History Available on Site

Fitters attending a machine for the first time cannot see past repairs, installed parts, or notes without phoning the back-office.

"If they've not visited before, they've got to rely on phoning somebody else to get the history."
BigChange Solution

Mobile Asset Register with Offline History

Every machine has a digital record. Fitters tap 'Job History' on their tablet to review past worksheets, replaced parts, operating hours, and photos—even when working offline in remote locations.

Current Bottleneck

Manual Routing for Safety Recalls

Back-office staff manually copy postcodes into Google Maps to visually group recalls and software campaigns across the UK.

"At the minute it's very much: what's the postcode, put it into Google Maps and sort of jigging around."
BigChange Solution

Geographic Clustering & Route Optimization

BigChange automatically groups outstanding safety recalls by territory and optimizes multi-stop routes based on fitter skills and vehicle cost—demonstrated live on the call, saving 31 miles and £85 on a single route.

Current Bottleneck

Untracked Van Consumables & Specialist Tools

Consumables (rags, oils, fasteners) and high-value company tooling on vans are untracked, causing cost leakage.

"Why is it Frank uses more rags than Jenny? Replenishing less often but buying more could save money."
BigChange Solution

Mobile Van Stock & Serialized Tool Audits

Consumables and parts decrement automatically when logged on worksheets, triggering low-stock reorder alerts. Company assets (jacks, test meters, torque wrenches) are assigned to vans and verified via digital audits.
